About

Mei is a professional violinist whose work begins with a deep classical foundation and moves outward — toward improvisation, new music, jazz, popular music, collaboration, and whatever sound comes next.

For Mei, genre is less a boundary than a vocabulary. The work is about sound, character, and connection: precision and freedom, intimacy and electricity, the written note and the moment that happens only once.
